Privacy Policy

Royal Aces

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Our Commitment to You

Royal Aces is built around a simple principle: your data stays on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. This Privacy Policy explains exactly what data lives on your device and what does not leave it.

Information We Do Not Collect

Royal Aces does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or behavior tracking
  • Advertiser identifiers (IDFA)
  • Device identifiers transmitted to third parties
  • Gameplay data, scores, or statistics submitted to any server
In plain language: Royal Aces makes no network calls related to your gameplay, profile, or preferences. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising network, no tracking pixel, and no crash reporter that transmits personal data.

Information Stored on Your Device

The following information is stored locally on your iPhone and is never transmitted off-device:

Profile Data

  • Your chosen avatar (either a custom photo you select or a built-in illustration)
  • Your selected card back design
  • App preferences (theme, sounds, haptics)

Gameplay Data

  • Current game state and saved games
  • Personal statistics (win rate, best score, best time)
  • Per-game play history
  • Achievement progress and unlocked badges

Camera and Photo Library

If you choose to set a custom avatar using the camera or your photo library, Royal Aces requests the corresponding iOS permission. The captured or selected image is stored locally on your device and is never uploaded, shared, or transmitted. You can revoke these permissions at any time through iOS Settings.

Data Storage and Security

All data is stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms within the app's sandboxed container. Data is protected by your device's built-in security features, including:

  • iOS app sandboxing (data is not accessible to other apps)
  • Device passcode, Touch ID, or Face ID protection (if enabled)
  • iOS Data Protection (when a device passcode is set)

Royal Aces does not use any third-party cloud storage, backup service, or remote database. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or iTunes, your Royal Aces data may be included in that backup according to your iOS backup settings, but Royal Aces itself does not initiate or control those backups.

Children's Privacy

Royal Aces does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected or transmitted, the app is safe for users of all ages. The app contains no advertising, no chat features, no user-generated content, and no external links that could lead children to inappropriate content.

Third-Party Services

Royal Aces does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app contains no SDKs that transmit user data. The app does not display any advertisements.

Your Rights and Choices

You have complete control over your data:

  • Reset All Data: The Profile tab in Royal Aces contains a one-tap Reset All Data option that permanently deletes all locally stored data, including your profile, statistics, achievements, and saved games.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling Royal Aces from your device permanently removes all app data.
  • Permissions: You can revoke camera and photo library permissions at any time through iOS Settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we ever update this Privacy Policy, we will post the revised version on this page and update the "Last updated" date at the top. Because Royal Aces does not collect any data, we do not have a way to notify you directly of changes. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
