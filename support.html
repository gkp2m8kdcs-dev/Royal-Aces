Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save my game?

Every game auto-saves the moment you make a move. You can close the app at any time and your current game, streak, and progress will be exactly where you left them when you return.

Where is my data stored?

All your data — profile, statistics, achievements, settings, saved games — is stored locally on your iPhone inside the app's sandboxed storage. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

How do I change my avatar?

Open the Profile tab, tap your current avatar, then choose to take a new photo with the camera or select one from your photo library. You can also pick from a set of built-in illustrations.

How do I unlock achievements?

Achievements unlock automatically as you play. For example, complete a game of Klondike Solitaire, hit a Blackjack, or run a five-streak in the Hand Trainer. Visit the Trophy Room in the Profile tab to see your progress.

How do I reset all my data?

Open the Profile tab, scroll to Settings, and tap Reset All Data. This will permanently delete your profile, statistics, achievements, and saved games. This action cannot be undone, so be sure before you tap.

Does Royal Aces require an internet connection?

No. Royal Aces is built for fully offline play. You can use it on a plane, in a tunnel, or anywhere else without service.

Does Royal Aces have ads or in-app purchases?

No. There are no advertisements and no in-app purchases in Royal Aces. The app is a one-time purchase with everything included.

How do the statistics work?

Royal Aces tracks your win rate, best score, best time, and per-game play history. All statistics are computed locally and stored on your device. Open the Profile tab to see your numbers.

What permissions does the app request?

Royal Aces may request access to the camera and photo library only if you choose to set a custom avatar. These permissions are entirely optional. The app will work fully without granting them.

Game Rules

If you want a refresher on the rules for any of the five games, open Royal Aces and tap the Game Rules page. Each game includes a quick-start guide and the rules you need.

Quick Links

  • Klondike Solitaire: Build four foundations from Ace to King by suit. Sort the tableau by alternating colors.
  • Pyramid Solitaire: Pair cards that sum to 13 (King alone, Queen + Ace, Jack + 2, etc.) to clear the pyramid.
  • Tri-Peaks Solitaire: Clear the three peaks by playing cards that are one rank higher or lower than the top of the stock pile.
  • Twenty-One: Beat the dealer by getting closer to 21 without going over. Hit, stand, or use basic strategy.
  • Hand Trainer: Identify the strongest poker ranking in a five-card hand. Build streaks to master the hierarchy.
