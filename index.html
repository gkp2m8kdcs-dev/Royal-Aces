Royal Aces

A Single-Player Card Club

Five timeless card games. One pocket-sized club. Zero distractions.

Welcome to the Table

Royal Aces is a single-player card club built for iPhone. No sign-up, no account, no network calls. Open the app, choose a table, and play. Everything you do is saved locally so you can pick up your streak tomorrow.

Built for offline play. No advertising. No tracking. No in-app purchase that affects gameplay outcomes. Your avatar, your statistics, your card back design, and your settings all live in the iPhone in your hand.

The Five Tables

♠ Klondike Solitaire

The patience game almost everyone has played — sort the tableau, build the foundations, clear the deck.

♦ Pyramid Solitaire

Rewards careful planning: pair cards that sum to thirteen and clear the layout.

♣ Tri-Peaks Solitaire

A faster flow with combo multipliers and a tap-the-difference mechanic that rewards quick eyes.

♥ Twenty-One

Free practice version of the casino classic — no chips, no stakes, no real money. Just you against the dealer.

♠ Hand Trainer

Five cards appear, identify the strongest poker ranking, and build up a streak. Royal Flushes are rare; High Card is common.

♦ The Library

A hand reference you'll actually use — every one of the ten poker hand rankings, with examples and the math behind the odds.

What's Inside

  • Trophy Room: Clear Klondike, hit a Blackjack, run a five-streak in the Trainer — unlock 12 achievements you can review any time.
  • Personal Statistics: Your win rate, best score, best time, and per-game history — all stored on your device.
  • Custom Profile: Choose your avatar, your card back design, and your settings. Make the club yours.
  • Complete Poker Reference: Suit and face-value pages give you quick reference for any card on the table.
  • Reset Anytime: The Profile tab has a one-tap Reset All Data option. Fresh start, whenever you want.
  • Always Offline: No internet connection required. Play on the plane, on the train, or anywhere life takes you.

A Club You Own

Royal Aces is a club you own, not a service you rent. Your data stays on your device. Your preferences stay with you. Your streak is yours to keep.

Whether you're sorting tableau in Klondike, pairing in Pyramid, racing the clock in Tri-Peaks, learning the math in the Trainer, or just unwinding with a quiet hand of Twenty-One — Royal Aces is ready when you are.
